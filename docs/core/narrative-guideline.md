# Narrative Guideline

## Purpose

KAMI MUSUBI does not provide absolute answers, fortune-telling, or spiritual certainty.

Narrative exists to help users:

- organize emotions
- understand their current state
- connect meaning to real-world movement
- take small reflective actions

The purpose of narrative is to support reflection, not to provide the "correct" answer.

---

# Narrative Principles

Every narrative should be based on the following principles.

## 1. Support, not conclusion

Narrative supports the user's own interpretation.

Avoid presenting a single correct answer.

## 2. Describe possibilities

Use expressions such as:

- "may"
- "might"
- "could be"
- "seems"

Avoid definitive statements.

## 3. Connect meaning

Narrative should explain why a shrine and the user's current situation can be connected.

The purpose is not persuasion, but understanding.

## 4. Encourage action

Narrative should gently encourage small real-world actions.

Examples include:

- taking a walk
- visiting a quiet place
- reflecting before making a decision

Never imply guaranteed outcomes.

---

# Input

Narrative may use:

- interpretation_profile
- translation_result
- Shrine Fact
- Shrine Meaning
- Recommendation Result

Narrative should not depend on UI state.

---

# Output

Narrative generates user-facing meaning such as:

- consultationSummary
- recommendationReason
- historyContext
- actionMeaning
- afterVisitReflection

The final wording is generated by the Composer.

---

# Prohibited Expressions

Narrative must not:

- diagnose psychological conditions
- predict the future
- provide religious certainty
- guarantee success
- claim a shrine will solve problems
- weaken the user's autonomy

Examples to avoid:

- "You are suffering from..."
- "This shrine will solve your problem."
- "This is the correct choice."
- "Your future will improve."

---

# Responsibility

Narrative is responsible for:

- translating structured meaning into natural language
- maintaining a calm and supportive tone
- connecting consultation themes with shrine context

Narrative is not responsible for:

- recommendation ranking
- score calculation
- UI presentation
- psychological diagnosis
- religious judgment
